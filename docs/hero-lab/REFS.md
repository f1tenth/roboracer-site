Hall lighting, for the prompts: daylight from the skylight strips plus rows of cool-white ceiling
practicals, even and nearly shadowless; the glossy grey-black floor reflects the orange tubes as
soft smears; white boxes cast no visible shadow; the LiDAR LEDs (green, blue) are the only accents.

# Research thumbnails and figures

Generated by `scripts/paper_thumbs.py`, all under `public/media/research/`, every file
checked by eye after generation (the review column records what was done by hand and
survives re-runs). Credit line on the site: "Figure: <first author> et al."
(PublicationCard).

- `research-<id>-1200.webp`: 1200x750 WebP under 120 KB, one per featured paper, the
  `thumbnail` field (the /research grid).
- `research-<id>-fig-1600.webp`: 1600x1000 WebP under 200 KB of one main figure of the
  paper, chosen by hand (`--size 1600 --figure n`, or `--pdf-page p --crop l,t,r,b` for
  papers without arXiv HTML), the `figure` field (the landing research carousel).
  Letterboxed on paper-50 unless the source says cover. Never a page-1 crop.

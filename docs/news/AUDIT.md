## Systemic issues

1. **Voice, length.** 52 of 53 excerpts run past the brief's 25-word card body (median 36, longest Columbia 2019 at 50, NSF 46, Montreal 48, ICRA 2021 workshop 45); the existing 22 items have a median of 11.5 after the copy pass. Titles run 6 to 12 words. Several excerpts repeat the headline's winner and event. A trim pass in the style of `docs/copy/data-news.md` is due before merge; facts above stay.
2. **Close paraphrase.** Rules repo (#5) near-verbatim; rename (#18) and the 2016 trailer wording (#53) stay close but are attributed or factual. No other excerpt copies its source.
3. **Date precision.** Consistent pattern: `date` = race (or last race) day, `date_display` = race or conference span, `day` precision. Choices to know: post-based cards use the event day, not the post day (VTC 2026 post Sep 21, ICRA 2023 post Jun 13, Germany 2022 post Aug 24); IROS 2023 uses Oct 5 though the race ran about Oct 4; the rules repo uses its creation date; the 2026 sim league uses the results-post date. Month precision only where sources conflict (Korea 2024, Foundation 2020): right.
4. **Tags.** Every `event` value exists in `events_map.json`. Ordinals in titles follow the map (2nd to 28th); London and San Antonio 2023 carry none. `course2024` is still `verified: false` in the map. Workshops stay untagged on purpose (the map's `icra2021-workshop` and `iros2023-madgames` ids would render raw through `eventLabel()`). The CDC 2025 tag on the sim-league item is fine because the league ran for CDC 2025; the item does not claim the in-person race.
5. **Status.** All 53 are `published`; `verify` renders identically, so wording carries the caution. Two items rest on one competitor's own post for their headline result (#4 ICCAS 2025, #36 Germany 2022): fixed by attribution above.
6. **Disputed details.** Leaks found: ICRA 2022 third place and bracket count (#37), and the TU Wien podium streak that depends on it (#32); the 2016 origin framing (#53). UNICORN = UNIST in #9 is now settled by the content skill's Teams section (Cedric, 2026-09-25) and TU Wien's page, so it is not a leak.
7. **Links.** tuwien.at/inf/scuderia-segfault/... served a Plesk "Scheduled Maintenance" page on a cookieless first request and the article on the second (CDC 2024 item #15 links there). Two links redirect (UNIST gallery to hmc.unist.ac.kr, UVA Today to archive.news.virginia.edu).

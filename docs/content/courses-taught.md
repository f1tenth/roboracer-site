# Where the RoboRacer course is taught, and the clubs around the car

Status: IN PROGRESS (2026-10-20, branch `content/courses-taught`). Research
for the "Taught on campus" section of `/learn/courses`. Nothing here is
published; every row is a candidate for Cedric to approve. Candidates in
machine-readable form: `docs/content/courses-taught.candidates.json`.

- Repo: `public/data/courses.json` (`offerings`: Penn published; TUM and UC
  San Diego `verify`), `public/data/news.json`, `public/data/testimonies.json`,
  `docs/content/teams.sheet.json` (RoboRacer_Teams_DB, 138 teams), the old
  f1tenth.org source (`_harvest/repos/f1tenth.github.io`) and the race-site
  repos in `_harvest/repos/`.
- arXiv 2209.11181, Betz et al., "Teaching Autonomous Systems Hands-On:
  Leveraging Modular Small-Scale Hardware in the Robotics Classroom" (2022;
  published as "F1TENTH: Enhancing Autonomous Systems Education Through
  Hands-On Learning and Competition", IEEE T-IV 2024): "Rosa Zheng, Joydeep
  Biswas, Krzystof Walas, Madhur Behl, and Venkat Krovi contributed to the
  syllabus setup, taught the course at their universities"; survey at four
  universities that taught it in spring 2022; course first taught 2018.
- Old f1tenth.org (live source mirrored in `_harvest/repos/f1tenth.github.io/index.html`):
  course material "used and taught by over 60 universities ... This includes
  universities such as University of California at San Diego, Clemson
  University, Oregon State University, University of Virginia, Lehigh
  University, University of Pennsylvania and many more."

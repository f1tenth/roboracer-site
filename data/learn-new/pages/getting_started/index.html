There is enough material for a full semester course, however if you have a limited amount of time, here are some suggestions:

  1. 4 Weeks - Go through Module A and Module B and complete Lab 1, Lab 2, Lab 3, and Lab 4. This will be enough to get your vehicle race ready.

  2. 10 Weeks (~1 Quarter) - Go through Module A, Module B, Module C, and Module D and complete Lab 1, Lab 2, Lab 3, Lab 4, Lab 5, and Lab 6. This will teach you how to race efficiently.

  3. 15 Weeks (~ 1 Semester) - Go through all of the Lectures and complete all of the Labs. This is the most comprehensive learning experience.

The RoboRacer Courses site offers these 4-, 10- and 15-week options as courses built on the modules and labs of this documentation, for students who want to take one and for instructors who want to teach it at their university.
